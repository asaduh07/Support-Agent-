Formula: escalation_score = 0.5 * escalated + 0.5 * min(days_since_created, 30) / 30   (older = higher)
Threshold: recommend escalation when score >= 0.75
Dataset: median days_since_created = 15.0, deciles = [2.0, 4.2, 6.3, 12.0, 15.0, 19.6, 23.0, 26.6, 28.9]
  0.75 = escalated (0.5) + age at the median (15/30 * 0.5 = 0.25), so it flags escalated tickets older than half the dataset: 8/50 = 16% of tickets.
  Non-escalated tickets max out at 0.5, so age alone never triggers it.
